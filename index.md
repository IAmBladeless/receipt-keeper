# Receipt Keeper Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 15, 2026
**Developer:** Unbladed
**Contact:** [gzilla1999@gmail.com](mailto:gzilla1999@gmail.com)

Receipt Keeper is an app for keeping receipts and tracking return windows and warranties. It was built so that your information never leaves your device unless you decide to move it yourself.

## The short version

- **We do not collect, store, sell or share any of your data.** We never receive it at all.
- The app has **no internet access**. It does not include the Android permission that would let it connect to the internet, so it cannot send anything anywhere.
- There are **no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting** in the app.
- Everything you enter stays on your device, in storage that only Receipt Keeper can read.

## Information you put in the app

When you add an item, you may enter a product name, store, category, price, purchase date, return window, warranty length and notes, and attach photos of receipts or products. This information:

- is stored only on your device, in Receipt Keeper's private app storage;
- is never sent to us or to anyone else by the app;
- is used only to show your items and to calculate return deadlines and warranty expiry dates.

## Photos

Receipt Keeper does not ask for camera or photo-library permission.

- **Take photo** opens your device's own camera app. The photo it takes is handed to Receipt Keeper and saved in the app's private storage.
- **Choose photos** opens the Android photo picker. Receipt Keeper receives only the photos you select, never the rest of your library.

Imported photos are resized and saved as new copies inside the app. Your original photos are not changed.

## Reminders and notifications

If you allow notifications, Receipt Keeper checks your items once a day **on your device** and shows a notification before a return window closes or a warranty ends. The check happens entirely on the device; nothing is sent anywhere. You can turn reminders off at any time in the app's Settings or in your device's settings.

## Backups and PDF exports

- **Export backup** creates a ZIP file of your items and photos and saves it **only where you choose** (for example, your device's Downloads folder or a cloud drive you select in the system file picker).
- **Share as PDF / Save as PDF** creates a PDF of one item and hands it to the app or location you choose.

These files are created only when you ask for them. If you save or share them to a third-party service (such as a cloud drive or email), that service's own privacy policy applies to the copy you send there.

Receipt Keeper excludes its data from Google's automatic cloud backup. When you set up a new phone and choose to copy data directly from your old phone (device-to-device transfer), Android may copy Receipt Keeper's data to the new phone.

## Purchases

Receipt Keeper Pro is a one-time purchase handled entirely by **Google Play**. Your payment details are collected and processed by Google, not by us; we never see them. To unlock Pro, the app asks the Google Play app on your device whether this Google account owns the Pro purchase. Google's handling of purchases is described in the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Permissions the app uses

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Notifications | To show return and warranty reminders, if you allow them. |
| Google Play billing | To let Google Play process the optional Pro purchase. |
| Run at startup, prevent sleep | Used by Android's background scheduler to keep the daily reminder check working, including after a restart. |

The app does **not** request internet, location, contacts, camera, microphone, or storage permissions.

## Children

Receipt Keeper is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

## Deleting your data

Because your data exists only on your device, you are always in control of it:

- Delete any item in the app; its photos are deleted with it.
- Clear the app's storage in your device settings, or uninstall the app, to delete everything.
- Backup or PDF files you exported are separate files; delete them wherever you saved them.

We cannot delete or recover your data for you, because we never have a copy.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date. Because the app has no internet access, it will never start collecting data silently; any change of that kind would require an app update with a new privacy policy and updated Google Play disclosures.
